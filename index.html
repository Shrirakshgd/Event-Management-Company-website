Our Story

The Thought Behind
Ashirwad Weddings & Events

"For me, hospitality is not just a service we offer; it is the feeling we leave people with. And that is the thought I want to bring into every wedding we curate."

I have always been someone who loves connecting with people, listening to their stories, understanding their needs, and making sure they feel heard and cared for. Hospitality was something I was naturally drawn to, and I always envisioned it as a part of my professional journey.

When I discovered the wedding industry and began understanding its many dimensions, I realised that there was something truly special about it. A wedding is not just about beautiful décor, grand celebrations, or perfectly planned functions. It is about people, emotions, experiences, and the little details that make every celebration memorable.

That is when I truly understood the importance of hospitality and logistics in a wedding. Everything may be beautifully planned, but it is exceptional hospitality and seamless logistics that bring the entire experience together. Making guests feel welcomed, comfortable, valued, and taken care of can elevate a wedding from a beautiful celebration to an unforgettable experience.

This understanding became the foundation of my journey into wedding management. I wanted to create experiences where every guest feels special, every requirement is understood, and every detail is taken care of with genuine warmth and attention.
